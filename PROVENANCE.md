# Provenance and attribution

## First-party workflow sources

- **paper-to-proposal** adapts ArdentL's first-party “论文收集、全文交叉阅读、Proposal与独立元评审” workflow, established 2026-11-03. The portable skill preserves actual model receipts, independent full-text reading, mechanism comparison, a falsifiable proposal and clean-context review.
- **research-decision-review** adapts ArdentL's first-party “AI for Science 科研协作工作流”, dated 2026-10-31. The public version replaces personal CLI paths, model versions and account-specific roles with configurable review and decision authority.
- Templates, packaging utilities and synthetic worked-example text are first-party material prepared for this repository. AI assistance was used for adaptation, documentation and validation.

The source documents are not copied wholesale and contain no dependency that users must obtain from the author's machine. Their identifiers and hashes are recorded in [release provenance](docs/release-provenance.json).

## Excluded content

No third-party skill text from Claude Scholar, Nature Skills, ARIS, Orchestra Research, K-Dense, Humanizer or other local installations is bundled or relicensed here. The repository contains neither real research PDFs nor private model-call records.

PlainSpeak is a separate project. Its writing skills and translation runtime are not included in this research-only bundle.
